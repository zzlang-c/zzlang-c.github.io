Research

My academic research focuses on large language models (LLMs), with an emphasis on improving their reasoning capabilities, building more effective LLM agents for complex tasks, and exploring their potential in healthcare applications. My background is mainly in natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML). My previous research experience spans various areas, including LLM Reasoning, LLM & RAG, LLM for Table Understanding, AI for Health, LLM Agent, NLP Applications, as well as other topics related to LLMs and ML.

bibtex / paper / code
